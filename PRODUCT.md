# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Young professionals (25–35) who are relocating or switching rentals. They are time-poor, phone-shy, and frustrated by the repetitive back-and-forth of scheduling property viewings. They already know what neighborhood and budget they want; the hard part is getting a viewing booked without chasing landlords.

## Product Purpose

An AI rental assistant that interviews the renter, matches them to properties, and books viewings automatically. The chatbot handles the conversation; a dashboard shows the renter exactly what is happening in the background (matches found, outreach sent, viewings confirmed).

Success is a booked viewing with zero manual emails or phone calls from the renter.

## Brand Personality

Friendly, fast, trustworthy.

- **Friendly:** conversational, never robotic; celebrates small wins like a confirmed viewing.
- **Fast:** every interaction feels instant; no long forms, no waiting for landlords to reply during the demo.
- **Trustworthy:** transparent about what the bot is doing — the dashboard shows every outreach and its status.

## Anti-references

- Corporate real-estate portals (Zillow, Rightmove) — dense listings, noisy ads, no automation.
- Spammy lead-gen chatbots that ask 20 questions then sell the lead.
- Phone-call-heavy concierge services that still require human scheduling.

## Design Principles

1. **Show the work:** the dashboard should make the automation visible, not hidden behind a chat bubble.
2. **One conversation, one outcome:** the chat narrows to a viewing, not an open-ended search.
3. **Trust through transparency:** every outreach to a landlord is logged and editable by the user.
4. **Demo-ready defaults:** fake or mocked external integrations (voice, email, WhatsApp) degrade gracefully so the flow always completes on stage.
5. **Speed over completeness:** the hackathon version proves the booking loop; extra features (payments, leases, maps) are out of scope.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA target. Clear focus states, readable contrast, reduced-motion alternatives, and screen-reader labels on all chat and dashboard elements.
